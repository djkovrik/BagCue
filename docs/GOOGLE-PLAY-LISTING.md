### Full description

> Pack for work, the gym, the pool, classes, or a busy day without relying on memory. BagCue turns your reusable packing kits into one clear checklist for the day, organized by bag.
>
> Plan in the evening, continue in the morning
>
> Create a packing session for today, tomorrow, or any future date. Check off items as you pack and return later without losing your progress.
>
> Combine the kits you need
>
> Select Office, Pool, or your own templates. BagCue merges them into a single daily packing list and avoids accidental duplicates. If the same item belongs in different bags, you can choose the right one before finishing.
>
> Know what goes where
>
> Group items by backpack, sports bag, or any label you create. Add a usual location to see helpful “Move from…” cues while packing.
>
> Make every checklist yours
>
> • Create, edit, duplicate, and delete packing templates
> • Maintain a reusable item catalog
> • Set quantities from 1 to 99
> • Add one-off items for today
> • Move or remove items without changing the original template
>
> Finish honestly
>
> Complete a fully packed list, or deliberately finish with skipped items and keep them visible in the result. Your history separates planned and completed sessions, and Repeat creates a fresh unchecked list for another date.
>
> Optional reminders
>
> Set evening and morning reminders for upcoming packing sessions. Notifications are optional-the checklist works without permission.
>
> Private by design
>
> Your packing items, templates, bag labels, and session history stay on your device. Core packing works offline and requires no account or cloud sync. Usage analytics is optional and off by default.
>
> BagCue includes editable Office and Pool starter kits, supports English and Russian, and follows your device’s light or dark theme.
